# OopsieHeroesNativeAndroid
Android version of OopsieHeroesNative (which is IOS)

Main Branch is desinged for android 13 or old versions. From android 14 or higher versions, the app cannot be installed from Google play store.

Main2 Branch is desinged for android 14 or any higher or lower versions. From android 14 or higher or lower versions, the app can be installed from Google play store.
